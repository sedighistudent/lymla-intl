# LYMLA — International Edition

Learn English with music, and prepare seriously for **IELTS** and **TOEFL iBT (2026 format)** — in one app.

## What's inside

- **IELTS Pro** — 10 full practice sets (Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking) with band-score estimates and AI writing feedback
- **TOEFL 2026 Pro** — 12 full sets in the new format: two-stage adaptive Reading & Listening, 1–6 scoring scale
- **Conversation Lounge** — 120 roleplay scenarios (IELTS, TOEFL, everyday life) with a live AI conversation partner and structured feedback after every session
- **Learn with songs** — tap any word for its meaning, save it to spaced-repetition review, shadow the singer, loop any line
- **Audiobooks** — classic English books with timed subtitles
- **Vocabulary & grammar** — thousands of words and a full A1–C1 grammar course

## Bring your own AI key (BYOK)

AI features (essay scoring, conversation partner, feedback) can run on **your own API key** — OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Mistral, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

Your key is stored **only on your device** and is sent only to the provider you choose. LYMLA servers never see it.

## Getting the app

Download the latest build from the **Releases** page of this repository and open it in your browser (it also installs as a PWA). Android builds are published as release assets as well.

## Activation

A licence code unlocks the premium sets. Codes are issued per device and verified with an offline digital signature, so the app keeps working without a connection.
